# Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Microsoft Excel analytics project designed to evaluate end-to-end supply chain efficiency, inventory health, supplier reliability, and fulfillment performance.

## Key Analysis Areas
* **Inventory Management:** Stock levels, reorder point alerts, carrying costs, and slow-moving vs. fast-moving SKU tracking.
* **Order & Fulfillment Tracking:** Order volume, fill rates, backorder trends, and processing lead times.
* **Delivery & Logistics Performance:** On-time delivery (OTD) rates, shipping delays, and carrier/route cost breakdowns.
* **Supplier & Material Analysis:** Vendor lead times, defect/return rates, and procurement spend across raw materials or product categories.

## Excel Techniques & Tools Used
* **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and merging raw operational tables.
* **Data Modeling & Formulas:** Relational lookups (`XLOOKUP`/`INDEX-MATCH`), conditional aggregations, and KPI calculations.
* **Pivot Tables & Interactive Dashboard:** Dynamic slicers, timeline filters, and KPI cards for executive reporting.
